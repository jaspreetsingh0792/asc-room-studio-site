ASC Room Studio

Try furniture made to order by ASC in a room you measure yourself, in daylight, at dusk and by candlelight. This is a concept prototype, not the launch website.

Two collections

  • The Veranda Set: weather-cured teak and hand-woven cane, in coastal daylight.
  • The Living Suite: linen seating, a carved credenza, ceramic and brass light, in a metro high-rise.

Flagship pieces are Price on request. Any other price shown is a sample, not a quote.

What the studio lets you do

  • Open a concept room, or set your own room's width, depth and height in metres.
  • Place pieces, then move, turn and resize them, and change their finishes.
  • Switch between Day, Dusk and Candlelight, or watch the light run from first light to lamplight.
  • View the room from a plan, or walk through it at eye height.
  • Save the design on your device, export a picture, or copy a read-only link for a client.
  • Request an estimate and send the design to ASC on WhatsApp.
